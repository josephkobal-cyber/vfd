# What Is an AI Receptionist? Answers, Guidance and a Human When Needed

An AI receptionist is a voice assistant that greets visitors at a physical front desk, answers their questions out loud, guides them through tasks like check-in, and brings in a person when it can't help. The Virtual Front Desk AI Agent does this on the lobby station. It talks with visitors, shows the conversation as text on screen, presses the station's buttons itself, and calls reception instead of guessing when it doesn't have a clear answer.

## How is an AI receptionist different from a chatbot or a phone bot?

A website chatbot answers typed questions from people who aren't in the building. An automated phone agent answers callers. An AI receptionist works with someone who is physically standing in your lobby, often in a hurry and sometimes unsure where to go.

That changes the job. It has to understand spoken questions in a noisy space, answer in a sentence or two, and either finish the task on the spot or put the visitor in front of a real person within seconds. A long, clever conversation is a failure at a front desk. A fast, correct next step is the goal.

## How the Virtual Front Desk AI Agent works

1. **The visitor steps up to the station.** The AI Agent notices someone in front of the camera and greets them with your custom greeting.
2. **The visitor asks a question.** They speak, and the AI Agent replies by voice. The whole conversation also appears in chat bubbles on the screen, so the visitor can read along. There's no typing.
3. **The AI Agent answers, guides or acts.** It answers what it knows, walks the visitor through steps such as check-in, and can press the station's buttons for them, for example to open the sign-in form or place a call.
4. **It hands off when needed.** When it doesn't have the answer, or the visitor asks for a person, it calls reception through the station's call buttons and the visitor continues on a live video call.
5. **It resets.** A few seconds after nobody is in front of the camera, the station returns to its welcome screen, ready for the next visitor.

## What does the AI receptionist know?

Out of the box, the AI Agent knows two things: every button on its station and what each one does, and the address of its location. Everything else comes from a context file you write and upload in the dashboard, as Markdown, text or PDF.

Think of the context file as the note you'd leave a new receptionist on their first day. The help center's [AI Agent setup guide](https://help.virtualfrontdesk.com/en/article/ai-agent-setup-guide) suggests nine topics, including who you are, when to use each button, how to get around the building, parking, hours, common questions, and the words your team uses. Keep it between 1 and 3 pages. Longer files make answers less precise.

Three rules keep it safe:

- **No greeting in the file.** The greeting is set separately in the station settings.
- **No tone instructions.** The AI Agent is always professional, and that can't be changed.
- **Nothing confidential.** Anything in the context file can be said out loud in your lobby, so door codes, passwords and personal phone numbers stay out.

## When does it hand off to a human?

Whenever it isn't sure. The Virtual Front Desk AI Agent is built not to guess. If a visitor asks something that isn't covered in its context file, it calls reception rather than improvising an answer. It also isn't designed for long conversations. Its job is to resolve simple requests quickly and move everything else to a person.

That's what "AI when it can, human when needed" means in practice. The AI Agent takes the repetitive questions, and a receptionist or employee takes the conversations that need judgment, over a live video call from the same station. Your context file can make the handoff rules explicit, for example: always call reception for deliveries, upset visitors, emergencies, or anyone asking for a specific person by name.

Video handoff uses the station's video call buttons, which are part of the Professional plan (from $49 per station per month) and the Enterprise plan.

## Does it speak other languages?

Yes. With the station's translator turned on, the visitor selects their language on the welcome screen, and the AI Agent speaks with them in that language. You choose which languages the station offers and can pin up to 3 of them for quick access. Your custom greeting is written in English and translated automatically.

The language carries over to the handoff. When the AI Agent brings in a person, live translated closed captions show the receptionist what the visitor says, in the receptionist's own dashboard language.

## Privacy and control

- **You decide whether conversations are saved.** With Save conversation history on, each conversation appears in History with a summary, labels, the number of turns and any handoff to a person. Turn it off if your policies don't allow transcripts.
- **Video calls are never recorded.** When the AI Agent hands a visitor to a person, the video call is live only.
- **It respects your hours.** When the station is closed by its office hours, the AI Agent doesn't greet visitors.
- **You control what it knows.** It only answers from its station's buttons, its location and the context file you approve.

## What to evaluate before deploying an AI receptionist

- Does it answer by voice and show the answer on screen?
- Can it take actions itself, such as starting check-in or placing a call, or only describe what to do?
- What does it do when it doesn't know the answer: guess, or hand off to a person?
- How quickly can a visitor reach a live human, and by what channel?
- Can it speak the visitor's language?
- Can you control what it knows, and keep confidential information out of it?
- Are conversations saved by default, and can you turn that off?
- Is it an add-on to the reception system you already use, or a separate product to integrate?

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between an AI receptionist and a virtual receptionist?

A virtual receptionist is usually a person who greets visitors remotely, often by video. An AI receptionist is software that answers common questions on its own. Virtual Front Desk combines both: the AI Agent handles simple requests, and a live person takes over by video when needed.

### Can the Virtual Front Desk AI Agent press buttons on the station?

Yes. The AI Agent knows every button on its station. It can guide the visitor through the screen or press the buttons itself, for example to open the sign-in form or to call reception.

### What happens if the AI Agent doesn't know the answer?

It doesn't guess. The Virtual Front Desk AI Agent calls reception whenever it doesn't have a clear answer, so the visitor continues with a live person on a video call from the same station.

### Which languages does the AI Agent speak?

The station's translator lets the visitor choose their language on the welcome screen, and the AI Agent then speaks with them in that language. Your custom greeting is translated automatically from English.
