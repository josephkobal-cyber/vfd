# Northgate Clinic — Look Bible v1

Status: **direction approved** (Higgsfield style frames A1 — reception wide, A3 — waiting area). 16:9. One look for every shot, so Higgsfield scenes and the
Synthesia Julia close-ups read as the same room.

## Mood in one line
A calm, premium, private clinic that feels closer to a boutique hotel lobby than a hospital:
warm, bright, quiet, uncluttered.

## Architecture
- Open reception lobby with a glass entrance door, waiting area visible behind reception.
- Soft architecture: rounded wall corners, one or two arched openings — borrowed from Julia's
  background, but restrained (no spiral staircase, no skylight dome).
- Large floor-to-ceiling windows on one side (natural daylight source).
- Light ceiling, recessed lighting, no fluorescent panels.

## Materials & palette

| Element | Material | Color |
|---|---|---|
| Walls | Smooth matte plaster | Warm white `#F4F0E8` |
| Reception counter | Light oak cabinet, rounded front corners | Oak `#C9A77C` |
| Counter top | Honed stone, soft rounded edge | Warm light grey `#D9D4CC` |
| Floor | Large-format stone / porcelain tiles, matte | Warm off-white `#E8E3DA` |
| Waiting chairs | Bouclé or linen upholstery, oak legs | Cream `#EDE6DA` |
| Plants | Olive tree, fiddle-leaf fig, trailing greenery in stone pots | Sage `#8A9A7B` |
| Accents | Thin brushed-brass details (sparingly) | `#B8975A` |
| Hero contrast | Neat Frame — black glass, graphite fabric base | `#1A1A1A` / `#4A4A4A` |

Julia's pale-yellow sweater is the only saturated color in frame — keep the set neutral so she
and the black station stand out.

## Lighting
- Soft late-morning daylight from the windows, camera-left in the reception shots.
- Warm, high-key, ~4500–5000K. Gentle shadows, no harsh contrast, no blown-out windows.
- Soft bounce fill so faces stay open — matches Julia's Synthesia lighting.
- Subtle sunlight patches on the floor/wall for depth (not dramatic god-rays).

## Camera language
- Cinematic, shallow depth of field (f/2–f/2.8 look), 35mm for wides, 50mm mediums, 85mm close-ups.
- Slow, smooth moves only: gentle dolly/track, locked-off for anything with a screen overlay.
- Clean, natural color grade, slight warmth, very fine film grain. No heavy teal/orange.

## Set dressing
- **Raised counter section:** the station sits on a raised light-oak block with the stone top (~105 cm), stepping up from the main counter (approved).
- **Reception counter:** only the Neat Frame and one small ceramic vase with a sprig of greenery.
  No papers, no pens, no monitors, no staff behind it (reception is unattended).
- **Waiting area:** 3–4 lounge chairs, low oak side table, a couple of neutral art books, one
  large abstract artwork in beige/sand/charcoal tones.
- **Clinic cues (subtle):** a corridor with light oak doors leading off the lobby, a discreet
  wall-mounted sanitizer, maybe a glimpse of a staff member in soft scrubs far in the background.
- **Background people:** at most 1–2 soft-focus people in the waiting area for life.

## Avoid
- Any readable text or signage (AI renders it badly) — no "Northgate Clinic" wall sign; if a
  sign is needed it's added in post.
- Hospital blue/green, fluorescent light, medical equipment, clutter, glossy luxury marble.
- Cool/grey office look, dark wood, strong color accents.
